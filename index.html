How to play

  1. Both teams start with 10 coins. Teams take turns, and the highlighted student answers for their team.
  2. Correct: pick 1 of 3 treasure chests. A chest either adds coins to your bank or steals coins from the other team.
  3. Wrong or out of time: the other team gets a rebound. If they answer it correctly, they steal 2 coins.
  4. Three correct in a row earns a hot streak bonus of 3 coins.
  5. When the clock hits 0:00, the question on screen is the final one. The team with the most coins wins.

Keyboard: 1–4 to answer, 1–3 to pick a chest, Enter for the next turn, P to pause.
